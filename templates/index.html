        Learn how regular expressions become automata

        This regex to DFA converter is also an automata theory visualizer. It shows how a regular expression becomes an epsilon-NFA, a DFA, and finally a minimized DFA.

        Thompson's construction

        The expression is first converted into an epsilon-NFA. Each symbol and operator contributes small state fragments connected by epsilon transitions.

        Subset construction

        Sets of NFA states are treated as DFA states. Epsilon-closure and symbol moves determine every transition in the resulting deterministic automaton.

        DFA minimization

        Equivalent DFA states are merged so the final machine accepts the same language with as few states as possible.

        Supported syntax

        Use letters and digits, | for union, adjacent symbols for concatenation, * for zero or more, + for one or more, and parentheses for grouping.

        Worked example: (a|b)*abb accepts strings over a and b that end in abb. Choose the example above to inspect its NFA, subset construction, minimized DFA, and transitions.
