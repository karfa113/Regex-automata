Turn a regular expression into the smallest DFA

Thompson's construction builds the NFA, subset construction turns it into a DFA, and minimization merges equivalent states. Type an expression, then test strings against the result.

Examples

Use letters and digits as symbols, | for union, * for zero or more, + for one or more, and ( ) to group. Writing two items side by side concatenates them.
